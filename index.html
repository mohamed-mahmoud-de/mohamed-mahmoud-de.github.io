Kidney-RAG

A clinical AI assistant that answers kidney-disease questions strictly from official medical guidelines, with citations.

Problem
Generic chatbots make up medical answers. Clinicians need responses they can trace back to KDIGO, NICE and USPSTF guidelines.
Built
Hybrid retriever (semantic + BM25 with weighted rank fusion) over 666 guideline chunks using MedEmbed-large in ChromaDB, served through FastAPI and deployed on Azure Container Apps. It refuses questions outside the guidelines.
Result
Top-5 retrieval accuracy of 0.867 and a perfect 1.0 faithfulness score on evaluation: answers stay grounded in the cited sources.

AirGuard

Forecasts Cairo's PM2.5 air pollution 6–12 hours ahead and turns each forecast into a health advisory.

Problem
Pollution spikes hit Cairo with little warning, and raw sensor numbers mean nothing to the public.
Built
Benchmarked ~40 models and selected XGBoost. MLflow handles champion-vs-challenger promotion. Airflow runs hourly inference and weekly retraining. Everything is Dockerized and forecasts map to EPA health categories.
Result
About 35% lower error (MAE) than the persistence baseline on a time-ordered holdout, running live with automated retraining.

07 — FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

How does payment work?

Fixed-price projects are split into milestones. You pay for each milestone after you've seen it working. We can work through Upwork or Contra for built-in protection, or direct invoicing via Payoneer.

Do I own the code?

Yes, 100%. Everything is delivered to your GitHub (or a repo I transfer to you) with setup documentation, so any engineer can run and maintain it.

What if something breaks after delivery?

Every project includes 14 days of free bug fixes after handover. Ongoing maintenance or monitoring is available as a small monthly retainer if you need it.

You're in Egypt. How do we communicate?

I work async by default (email, Slack, or Upwork messages) with updates every 2–3 days, and I'm online during US East Coast mornings for calls. Replies within 24 hours, usually much faster.

Can you work with my existing code or data?

Definitely. Fixing, extending or automating an existing script, spreadsheet workflow or pipeline is often the fastest win. Send me what you have and I'll tell you honestly what's worth keeping.
